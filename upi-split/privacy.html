Privacy Policy – UPI Split

Effective date: 4 November 2026

UPI Split (package dev.chandra.upi_split) is made by ChandraChaan Apps. It splits a bill between friends and creates a UPI payment link or QR code for each person.

Information we collect

None. The app has no account, no ads, no analytics and no tracking, and does not send your information to us or to anyone else. We do not collect, share or sell personal or device data.

Information stored on your device

Names, amounts and recent splits you enter are saved on your device. This data stays in the app's private storage on your phone and is removed when you clear the app's data or uninstall it.

Payments

UPI Split does not process, receive or store payments. It only creates standard UPI payment links (upi://pay). When you or a friend open a link, your chosen UPI app handles the payment under its own privacy policy. When you share a link, it goes only to the app you pick, such as a messaging app.

Children

The app collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
