Privacy Policy – UPI Split

Effective date: 20 November 2026

UPI Split (package dev.chandra.upi_split) is made by ChandraChaan Apps. It has two parts:

  • Bill splitting splits a bill between friends and creates a payment link or QR code for each person. It needs no account.
  • Shop (version 1.1.0 and later, and the Shop web app) helps a small shop keep its sales, customer dues (khata), stock, suppliers and expenses. It needs an account.

Bill splitting

Bill splitting collects nothing. It has no account, no ads, no analytics and no tracking, and it does not send your information to us or to anyone else.

Names, amounts and recent splits you enter are saved only on your device, in the app's private storage. They are removed when you clear the app's data or uninstall it.

UPI Split does not process, receive or store payments. For each friend it creates a link to a small web page (chandrachaan.github.io/upi-split) or a QR code. The payment details, such as your name, UPI ID and the amount, are inside the link's # fragment, which browsers never send to any server, so the page host cannot see them. When you share a link, it goes only to the app you choose, such as a messaging app. Payments happen entirely in your friend's UPI app, under that app's own privacy policy.

Shop: information we collect

Nothing is collected unless you open Shop and sign in. When you use Shop, we store:

  • Your account: your email address and password (handled by Google Firebase Authentication; we never see the password), your name, and your role in the shop.
  • Your shop's details: shop and store names, address, phone number, GSTIN, UPI ID and receipt text.
  • Your customers' details that you enter: names, phone numbers, addresses, credit limits, dues, and the record of credit purchases, payments and reminders.
  • Your business records: bills, products, prices, stock, suppliers, purchases, payments to suppliers, expenses and daily totals.
  • Staff: names and email addresses of people you invite, and which entries each person made.

We do not collect your location, contacts, photos, device identifiers or advertising ID, and Shop has no ads or analytics.

Shop: how the data is used and shared

  • The data is used only to run Shop for you and the staff you invite, for example to show dues, print receipts and add up the day's sales.
  • It is stored with Google Firebase (Cloud Firestore and Firebase Authentication), which processes it for us. It is encrypted in transit (HTTPS) and at rest.
  • Only people you invite to your shop can see its data. We do not sell or share it with anyone else, and we do not use it for advertising.
  • A copy is kept in the app's private storage on each phone so Shop works offline.
  • When you send a customer a reminder, it goes through WhatsApp or SMS on your own phone, under those apps' privacy policies.

You are responsible for having your customers' permission to record their details and dues in Shop.

Keeping and deleting your data

We keep Shop data while your account is active. You can delete your account in the app under Shop → More → Settings → Delete account, or by email. See how to delete your account and data.

  • If you own the shop, we erase the shop and all its records within 30 days.
  • If you are staff, your login is deleted. Entries you made stay in the owner's records, because they are the shop's books.

Children

UPI Split is not directed at children. Bill splitting collects no data from anyone. Shop is meant for shop owners and their staff.

Changes
