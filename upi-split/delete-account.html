UPI Split: Bill Splitter – delete your Shop account and data

Developer: ChandraChaan Apps. This page explains how to delete your UPI Split Shop account and the data linked to it. Bill splitting has no account; its data stays on your phone and is removed when you uninstall the app.

Delete in the app

  1. Open UPI Split and tap Shop.
  2. Go to More → Settings.
  3. Tap Delete account, enter your password and confirm.

Your login is deleted straight away.

Delete by email

If you no longer have the app, email chandrachaan.apps@gmail.com from the address you use to sign in, with the subject "UPI Split account deletion". We delete the account within 30 days and reply to confirm.

What is deleted and what is kept

  • Shop owners: your login is deleted, and within 30 days we erase your shop and all its records: customers, khata entries, bills, products and stock, suppliers, expenses, staff list and invites. Staff you invited lose access.
  • Staff: your login is deleted. The entries you made, and your name on them, stay in the shop owner's records, because they are the shop's books. The owner can ask us to erase the whole shop.
  • We keep no copies after deletion.
  • Data on your phone, such as saved bill splits and Shop's offline copy, is removed when you clear the app's data or uninstall it.
