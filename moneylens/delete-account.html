MoneyLens: Expense Tracker – delete your account and data

Developer: ChandraChaan Apps. This page explains how to delete your MoneyLens cloud backup account and the data linked to it.

Delete in the app

  1. Open MoneyLens.
  2. Go to Backup & export → Cloud backup.
  3. Tap Delete cloud data & account and confirm.

This deletes your cloud backup account (email address and sign-in details) and every cloud backup stored for it, straight away.

Delete by email

If you no longer have the app, email chandrachaan.apps@gmail.com from the address you used for cloud backup, with the subject "MoneyLens account deletion". We delete the account and all its backups within 30 days and reply to confirm.

What is deleted and what is kept

  • Deleted: your account email and sign-in record, and all encrypted cloud backups.
  • Not kept: we keep no copies of your account or backups after deletion.
  • Data on your phone is separate and never uploaded unless you use cloud backup. To remove it, use Settings → Privacy → Delete all data on this phone, or uninstall the app.
