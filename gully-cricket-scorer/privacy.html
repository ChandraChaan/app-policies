Privacy Policy – Gully Cricket Scorer

Effective date: 4 November 2026

Gully Cricket Scorer (package io.github.chandrachaan.gully_cricket_scorer) is made by ChandraChaan Apps. It keeps ball-by-ball scores for street, school and weekend cricket matches.

Information we collect

None. The app has no account, no ads, no analytics and no tracking, and does not send your information to us or to anyone else. We do not collect, share or sell personal or device data.

Information stored on your device

Team names, player names and match scores you enter are saved on your device. This data stays in the app's private storage on your phone and is removed when you clear the app's data or uninstall it.

Sharing

When you share a scorecard, it goes only to the app you choose.

Children

The app collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
