Privacy Policy – Tambola Host

Effective date: 4 November 2026

Tambola Host (package io.github.chandrachaan.tambola_host) is made by ChandraChaan Apps. It helps you host a Tambola (Housie) game: it calls numbers aloud, makes printable tickets and checks claims.

Information we collect

None. The app has no account, no ads, no analytics and no tracking, and does not send your information to us or to anyone else. We do not collect, share or sell personal or device data.

Information stored on your device

Your game settings, current game and generated tickets are saved on your device. This data stays in the app's private storage on your phone and is removed when you clear the app's data or uninstall it.

Speech and printing

Numbers are spoken with your phone's built-in text-to-speech engine. When you print or share tickets, the document is handed to the printer or app you choose.

Children

The app collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
