Privacy Policy – PosePerfect

Effective date: 4 November 2026

PosePerfect (package com.chandrachaan.pose_perfect) by ChandraChaan Apps is an AI camera that suggests poses and colour looks for where you are. This policy explains what the app collects and why. We do not sell your data or use it for advertising.

Camera and photos

The camera preview stays on your phone. Photos you save go to a "PosePerfect" album on your phone. If you tap AI Enhance, that one photo is sent to Google's Gemini model (through Google Firebase) to get colour-correction suggestions and tips. We do not store your photos on our servers.

Location

If you allow location, the app reads your position to recognise the kind of place (beach, city, heritage site and so on) and to work out the light from the sun's position. To suggest poses, the place name and your coordinates (rounded to about 100 m) are sent to Google's Gemini model through Firebase. Location is not saved with your photos or in our database. You can deny or turn off location; the app then uses on-device suggestions.

Account and saved choices

The app signs you in anonymously with Firebase Authentication, which gives the app a random user ID with no name or email. When you save a photo, the creative choices behind it (filter, adjustments, scene type, light, pose and AI score, never the photo or your location) are stored in Google Cloud Firestore under that ID, to improve suggestions.

Analytics and crash reports

The app uses Google Analytics for Firebase to count how features are used (for example which pose or filter you picked) and Firebase Crashlytics to receive crash reports. These include app and device information and Firebase identifiers.

Service providers

All of the above is processed by Google (Firebase, Gemini API, Analytics and Crashlytics) on our behalf, under Google's privacy policy. Data is sent over encrypted connections.

Deleting your data

Uninstalling the app removes everything stored on your phone. To delete the saved choices and analytics linked to your app's anonymous ID, email us from the device's account with the subject "PosePerfect data deletion" and we will delete them within 30 days.

Children

PosePerfect is not directed at children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
