Privacy Policy – Notification Batch

Effective date: 4 November 2026

Notification Batch (package io.github.chandrachaan.notification_batch) by ChandraChaan Apps holds notifications from apps you pick and delivers them together a few times a day.

Information we collect

None. The app has no internet permission, so it cannot send anything off your phone. There are no ads, no analytics, no crash reporting and no account. We do not collect, share or sell any data.

Notification access

To hold a notification the app must see it arrive, which Android calls "notification access". With it, the app reads notifications on this phone and removes the ones from apps you chose to batch from the shade, to deliver them later. It only holds notifications from the apps you choose. You can turn access off at any time in Android settings.

Information stored on your device

Held notifications are stored in the app's private storage and are excluded from cloud backup and device transfer. Delivered items are kept for two days so you can find them in the app. Stats are daily counts per app, kept for five weeks, and never include what a notification said. Uninstalling the app deletes everything it stored.

Contacts (optional)

If you turn on "Starred contacts", the app asks for contacts access to let messages from people you starred come through right away. Contacts are read on the phone only and are never stored or sent anywhere.

Children

The app collects no data from anyone, including children.
