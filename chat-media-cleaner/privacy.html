Privacy Policy – Chat Media Cleaner

Effective date: 4 November 2026

Chat Media Cleaner (package dev.chandra.chat_media_cleaner) by ChandraChaan Apps helps you find and remove large, duplicate and old media files saved by chat apps on your phone.

Information we collect

None. The app has no internet permission, so it cannot send anything off your phone. There are no ads, analytics, crash reporting or accounts. We do not collect, share or sell any data.

How the app uses your files

You choose a media folder with Android's folder picker, and the app gets access to that folder only. A scan reads file names, sizes and dates, and compares file contents on the phone to find exact duplicates. Photos and videos are opened only to show thumbnails and previews. Files are moved to the trash or deleted only when you choose them. You can take back folder access at any time in the app's settings or in Android settings.

Information stored on your device

Settings and the list of files in the app's trash are kept in the app's private storage. Uninstalling the app removes them.

Children

The app collects no data from anyone, including children.
