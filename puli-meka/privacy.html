Privacy Policy – Puli Meka: Tiger & Goats

Effective date: 4 November 2026

Puli Meka (package com.chandrachaan.pulimeka) is a free, offline board game made by ChandraChaan Apps.

Information we collect

None. The app has no account, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. It does not collect, upload, share or sell any personal or device information.

Information stored on your device

Your settings and game statistics are saved only on your device and never leave it. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data removes them.

Permissions

The app requests no sensitive Android permissions and works offline.

Links

Optional links on the About screen (share, report a problem on GitHub, source code) open other apps or your browser only when you tap them.

Children

The app collects no data from anyone, including children.
