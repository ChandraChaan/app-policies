Privacy Policy – Daily Reminder

Effective date: 5 November 2026

Daily Reminder (package dev.chandra.medicine_reminder) by ChandraChaan Apps rings like an alarm at reminder time and reads the reminder out in Telugu, Hindi or English.

Information we collect

None. The app has no internet permission, no account, no ads, no analytics and no crash reporting. We do not collect, share or sell any personal or device data.

Information stored on your device

Reminders, schedules, photos you add to reminders, reminder history and settings are stored only in the app's private storage on the phone. Android's automatic app backup is turned off for this app, so this data is not copied to a cloud backup. Uninstalling the app deletes everything.

Photos

If you add a photo to a reminder, the app uses Android's photo picker or camera for that one photo and keeps a copy in its private storage. It does not read your other photos.

Optional family check

The family check is off until you turn it on. When a reminder is missed, a notification on the phone opens your SMS app or WhatsApp with a message ready, and nothing is sent until someone presses Send. The message contains only the name you typed, the reminder name and the time. The family phone numbers you enter are stored only on the phone.

Children

The app collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
