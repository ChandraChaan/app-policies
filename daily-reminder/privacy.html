Privacy Policy – Voice Saathi

Effective date: 15 November 2026

Voice Saathi (package dev.chandra.medicine_reminder, previously called Daily Reminder) by ChandraChaan Apps is a phone helper for elderly parents. You press one button and speak in Telugu, Hindi or English to call family, hear the time, the date, today's reminders or the battery level, or turn on the torch. Reminders ring like an alarm and are read out.

Information we collect

None. The app has no internet permission, no account, no ads, no analytics and no crash reporting. We do not collect, share or sell any personal or device data.

Information stored on your device

Reminders, schedules, reminder history, family members (names, phone numbers, other names and photos you add) and settings are stored only in the app's private storage on the phone. Android's automatic app backup is turned off for this app, so this data is not copied to a cloud backup. Uninstalling the app deletes everything.

Microphone and speech recognition

The app listens only after the big talk button (or "Say it" in Family setup) is pressed, and stops when you stop speaking. It does not record or keep any audio.

Speech is turned into text by Android's own speech recognition service, not by the app. On phones that can recognise the chosen language on the device, only the on-device recogniser is used and the audio does not leave the phone. If the phone cannot recognise the language on the device, listening stays off unless the family turns on Allow online listening in Family setup. Then the phone's speech service (usually Google's) sends the audio to its servers to turn it into text, under that service's own privacy policy, for example the Google Privacy Policy. The app only receives the resulting text, uses it to decide what to do, and does not store or send it.

Calls

When you tap a family member's photo, say their name or press Help, the app places a call to the number the family saved, using the phone's own dialer. With the Phone permission it calls straight away; without it, the dialer opens with the number filled in. The app does not read your call log or contacts.

Photos

If you add a photo to a reminder or a family member, the app uses Android's photo picker or camera for that one photo and keeps a copy in its private storage. It does not read your other photos.

Optional family check

The family check is off until you turn it on. When a reminder is missed, a notification on the phone opens your SMS app or WhatsApp with a message ready, and nothing is sent until someone presses Send. The message contains only the name you typed, the reminder name and the time. The family phone numbers you enter are stored only on the phone.

Children

The app collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
